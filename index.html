📖 The Bookkeeper's Rule on Leftover Cents

Every expense is divided in whole cents. When an amount cannot be divided into three equal integer cents (such as a $100.00 dinner, where 10,000¢ ÷ 3 = 3,333¢ with 1 leftover cent), the leftover cents are assigned to the participants in roster order: Linda, then Dave, then Carol.
For instance, a $100 dinner paid by Linda splits $33.34 to Linda, $33.33 to Dave, and $33.33 to Carol. Because Linda paid $100.00, she gets back $66.66 ($100.00 − $33.34), while Dave and Carol each owe $33.33. Every single cent is strictly accounted for.
